# Floom

> Floom is a curated online marketplace for flowers and plants that connects customers with a vetted network of independent local florists, enabling same-day and next-day delivery of designer bouquets, arrangements, and gifts across the United Kingdom and United States. Floom routes each order to a nearby artisan florist rather than operating central warehouses, and also provides florists with e-commerce, ordering, and fulfillment technology.
